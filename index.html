THE CHALLENGE

Great events need more than good planning.

Behind every successful live event is a team working to keep everything on track. But when timing, communication, and transitions fall out of sync, the whole programme can feel the pressure.

When time slips away

Speakers run over their allocated time, sessions stretch longer than planned, and the rest of the programme gets pushed behind schedule.

When teams lose sync

Speakers, organizers, and media teams need to stay aligned. Without clear coordination, important updates can be missed when they matter most.

When transitions get messy

Unexpected delays and programme changes can make it harder to move smoothly from one speaker, session, or activity to the next.

The goal isn't just to run an event. It's to help everyone involved stay on the same page.

WHAT WE'RE BUILDING

Meet the timer built for moments that matter.

We're starting with a dedicated desktop timer to help speakers and event teams stay aware of time during sermons, presentations, conferences, and other live events.

HOW IT WORKS

A simpler way to stay aware of time.

Every event runs on a schedule. Tymly's timer is designed to help speakers and event teams keep track of time as each session unfolds.

01

Choose the session duration

Set the time allocated to the session so you have a clear target to work toward.

02

Start the countdown

Begin the timer when the session starts and keep the allocated time in view.

03

Keep the programme on track

Use the remaining time as a reference to help manage the session and stay mindful of the schedule.

Small tool. Important role. Better time awareness can help everyone involved approach each session with greater clarity.
