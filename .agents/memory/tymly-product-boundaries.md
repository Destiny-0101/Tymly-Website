name: Tymly product boundaries
description: Current Tymly capabilities and the distinction between them and the longer-term event coordination vision.
---

Public Tymly messaging should describe only the current desktop countdown timer: preset durations, a large visible countdown, and a simple operator flow. The broader event coordination platform—including programme management, team roles, speaker views, notifications, live changes, multi-screen support, and integrations—is future vision unless later confirmed as implemented.

Do not reuse customer logos, testimonials, performance metrics, or contact details from the Figma screenshot as verified facts without confirmation.

**Why:** The product brief explicitly separates the current timer from future possibilities, while the visual reference includes claims and contact details that are not independently verified.

**How to apply:** Use this boundary when writing Tymly website, product, and download messaging; label planned capabilities as future vision and omit unsupported proof.
